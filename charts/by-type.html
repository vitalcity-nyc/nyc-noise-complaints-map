Sound and the city

Half of all 311 noise calls are about your neighbors

Residential noise — music, parties, banging, a television through the wall — accounts for half of every 311 noise complaint in the city. Street and sidewalk noise is a distant second at 24 percent. Helicopters are under 3 percent.
